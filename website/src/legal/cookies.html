Cookies & browser storage

Your browser, your choice. Optional storage starts off.

No advertising or analytics cookies

The website’s own code sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising SDKs. Cookies set by the hosting platform, if any, are governed by that platform’s policies. The dashboard’s session cookie belongs to the Snag application, not this information site.

What can be stored

ItemPurposeDuration
snag-site:settings:v1Records the storage choice you explicitly save, whether appearance should be remembered, and whether offline copies are enabled.Up to 180 days; removable in settings.
snag-site:theme:v1System, light, or dark appearance. Written only when Remember appearance is enabled.Until the saved choice expires or you clear it.

Default behaviour

The site opens in a dark appearance. Theme changes work immediately for the current page without permission to remember them. Offline caching is disabled until you enable it. Choosing Use defaults records only that storage choice so the notice does not repeatedly appear. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the notice is not shown and optional storage stays off unless you turn it on in the settings.

Consent record

A saved choice records the options you selected, the policy version, and the date it was saved. When this notice changes in a way that affects storage, the policy version changes and you are asked again.

Manage your choice

Use the footer’s Cookie & storage settings to change or withdraw optional storage. Disabling offline copies unregisters this site’s worker and removes its named caches. Clear offline cache removes those copies and turns offline mode off. Reset all preferences also removes the saved settings and restores the default dark appearance.

Ordinary browser caching

Your browser and host may cache network responses independently of the optional offline feature. This site cannot erase the browser’s entire HTTP cache, hosting cookies, or data from another application. Use your browser’s site-data controls for those.

Changes to this notice

This page is updated whenever the website’s use of browser storage changes. The consent design follows the ICO guidance on managing consent.
